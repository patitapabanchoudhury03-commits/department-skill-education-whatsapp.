# Privacy Policy
**Department of Skill Education WhatsApp Survey Agent**

**Last updated: 3 November 2026**

## 1. About this survey

The Department of Skill Education uses this WhatsApp survey to understand the employment status, skill areas, and job-alert preferences of students and trainees. The survey is intended to support skill development and industry-connect initiatives.

## 2. Information we collect

When you participate in the survey, we may collect:

- Your name
- Gender, including the option to prefer not to disclose
- Current status, such as employment, self-employment, studying, training, or not currently working
- Your preference for receiving job-related information through WhatsApp
- Your trade or skill area
- Your WhatsApp phone number
- Your survey progress and response status

## 3. How we use your information

Information collected through the survey may be used to:

- Understand student and trainee employment status and skill areas
- Support skill development planning and industry partnerships
- Understand interest in receiving job-related information
- Record and manage survey responses

Job-related messages should be sent only where appropriate and consistent with the participant's stated preferences.

## 4. WhatsApp and service providers

The survey is conducted through WhatsApp. WhatsApp and Meta may process information in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies.

Survey responses may be stored in a database used by the survey application. Access should be limited to authorised personnel who need the information for the survey's stated purposes.

## 5. Data retention

Survey information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy and applicable organisational requirements. The Department should establish and follow an appropriate retention and deletion schedule.

## 6. Data security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect survey information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Your choices and requests

Participation in the survey is voluntary. You may choose not to answer a question or stop participating.

For questions about your information, or to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, contact the Department of Skill Education through its designated official contact channel.

## 8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the survey or its data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published at this page.

## 9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the Department of Skill Education through its designated official contact channel.
